\begin{abstract}
The goal of this week is to form your teams and establish how you will work together over the semester.  Team-Based Learning relies on stable, well-functioning teams --- the time you invest here pays off in every subsequent week.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Topics}
\begin{description}
\item[Team formation] How teams are composed and the rationale behind stable semester-long teams
\item[Team contracts] Agreeing on expectations, communication, and conflict resolution
\end{description}

\section*{Preparation}
There is no preparation required for Week 1.  The first class is devoted to introductions and team formation.


\section*{EPIC activity}

Your task this week is to create a \emph{team contract}.  Once teams are created, the team will draw up a team contract which each team member records in their log book.  The team contract should spell out the following:
\begin{itemize}
\itemsep0em
\item The team name
\item The team members
\item The expectations of each member
\item The conditions under which a member may be excluded
\item Communication rules and preferred channels
\item Anything else you think will help the team work well together
\end{itemize}

We recommend team sizes of 5-6.

\subsection*{Reporting}

This is a \emph{log book} style class.  In the reporting period, take 25 minutes to fill in a page of your log book and show it to the teacher during class time.  The teacher gives feedback on the spot and signs off the log book.  Record things like:
\begin{itemize}
\itemsep0em
\item Your team name and members.
\item The key terms of your team contract.
\item Any concerns or questions you have about the semester ahead.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Before you go}

Before you leave, make sure your computer is ready for next week: Git must be installed and you need a GitHub account you can log in to.  Next week's activity relies on every team member being able to push and pull to a shared repository, so sort this out now rather than struggling during class.  If you haven't done this before, follow the \href{https://docs.github.com/en/get-started/git-basics/set-up-git}{GitHub setup guide}\footnote{\url{https://docs.github.com/en/get-started/git-basics/set-up-git}}.
