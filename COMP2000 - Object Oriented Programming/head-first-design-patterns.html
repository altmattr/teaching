the decorator pattern The Decorator Pattern defined Let’s first take a look at the Decorator Pattern description: The Decorator Pattern attaches additional responsibilities to an object dynamically. Decorators provide a flexible alternative to subclassing for extending functionality. While that describes the role of the Decorator Pattern, it doesn’t give us a lot of insight into how we’d apply the pattern to our own implementation.
